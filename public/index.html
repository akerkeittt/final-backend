Singapore

Singapore, officially the Republic of Singapore, is an island country and city-state in Southeast Asia. It consists of one main island, 63 satellite islands, and one outlying islet. Singapore is located near the equator, off the southern tip of the Malay Peninsula.

Facts about Singapore

  • 🌿 Singapore is one of the world's greenest cities.
  • 🌊 It has the world's tallest indoor waterfall.
  • ✈️ Changi Airport has been rated the best airport for years.
